## Brand & Style

The design system is engineered for driven, everyday achievers seeking daily momentum without cognitive fatigue. It translates productivity from a chore into a rewarding, tactile ritual. The emotional tone is inviting, high-momentum, and celebratory—balancing structured clarity with gamified warmth.

The design movement blends **Tactile Modernism** with **Playful Utility**:
- Soft, warm-tinted atmospheric backdrops that eliminate sterile screen glare.
- Cheerful, high-contrast action points driving clarity through purposeful color assignment.
- Volumetric depth using soft, warm drop shadows and subtle border-stroke definitions, giving interactive items an almost physical, pushable feel.
- Generous internal padding and fluid geometric radii to evoke approachability, ease, and sustained enthusiasm.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate dopamine feedback loop using saturated, warm focal points balanced against soft base surfaces.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF7F2`):** A soft, buttery cream canvas serving as the default viewport background. Eliminates harsh blue-white glare.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white container base for actionable cards and modal sheets, providing natural separation from the cream canvas.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#F4EFEA`):** Recessed containers, inactive inputs, and segmented controls.
- **Border / Divider (`#E8DFD5`):** Warm structural lines maintaining card boundaries with low visual friction.

### Typography & Content
- **Text Primary (`#27272A`):** High-contrast neutral charcoal for crisp reading across all scales.
- **Text Secondary (`#716E69`):** Grounded warm slate for timestamps, subheadings, and secondary metadata.
- **Text Inverted (`#FFFFFF`):** High-legibility text on colored badges, primary action triggers, and active states.

### Accent Roles
- **Primary Coral (`#FF5D38`):** Urgency, high priority, dynamic action triggers, and the primary focus ring.
- **Mint Emerald (`#10B981`):** Task completions, check state animations, and positive metric graphs.
- **Playful Purple (`#8B5CF6`):** Project categories, contextual tags, and deep-focus milestones.
- **Sunshine Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Streaks, fire rewards, badge accolades, and daily check-in celebrations.

## Typography

The system utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** throughout the entire typographic hierarchy. Its open apertures, geometric foundational forms, and contemporary tall x-height make it readable at small sizes while remaining upbeat and friendly in heavy display weights.

- **Weight Intent:** Titles and numerals demand high visual punch (`700` and `800` weights) to highlight completion counts and streak counts. 
- **Body Styling:** Standard tasks lean on weight `500` for higher legibility on warm canvas backings compared to thin weights.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Use tabular numbers where applicable (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) across timers, counts, and streak dials to prevent horizontal jitter during updates.

## Layout & Spacing

A 4-column fluid mobile grid anchored by an 8-point base metric rhythm. On tablets and desktop companion views, the grid scales to 8 and 12 columns respectively, with an absolute maximum content width capped at `680px` for focused single-column task workflows or `1024px` for split-view dashboards.

- **Mobile Viewport (< 600px):** Single-column stack with `1.25rem` outer canvas padding (`margin`). Task items maintain a standard minimum touch target height of `56px`.
- **Tablet / Expanded Viewport (600px - 1024px):** Dual-pane structure (Sidebar navigation / Task Feed) utilizing `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` outer padding.
- **Desktop Viewport (> 1024px):** Centered workspace view keeping tasks directly within natural eye span.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Tight list rows (`space-sm` gap between related subtasks), standard grouping separation (`space-lg`), and thematic milestone separation (`space-xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Tinted Tactile Elevation** rather than stark, cold drop shadows. Depth mimics soft ambient daylight cast over tactile cards.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** `#FAF7F2` - Default canvas, zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Resting Task Cards):** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8DFD5`, box shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(92, 70, 52, 0.05), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(92, 70, 52, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered / Floating Action Items):** Box shadow: `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(92, 70, 52, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(92, 70, 52, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Overlays / Bottom Sheets):** Background `#FFFFFF`, box shadow: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(39, 39, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(39, 39, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Gamified Elements (Streak / Completion Triggers):** Colored glow accents mirroring the trigger color: `0 8px 20px -2px rgba(255, 93, 56, 0.28)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a volumetric, friendly aesthetic via large, pill-shaped and squircle radiuses. 

- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Used universally for chips, tag badges, status markers, segmented control thumbs, floating navigation pills, and primary CTAs.
- **Large Cards & Sheets (`1.5rem` - `2rem` / `24px` - `32px`):** Used for parent task cards, stats overviews, and bottom modal drawers.
- **Inner Controls (`0.75rem` - `1rem` / `12px` - `16px`):** Checkboxes, form textfields, and inner nested cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), primary coral (`#FF5D38`) fill with pure white label text (`label-lg`). Includes a tactile bottom edge transition: active state translates downward `2px` with a subtle inset shadow to deliver physical haptic feedback.
- **Secondary Action:** Pill-shaped, surface cream (`#F4EFEA`) background with charcoal text (`#27272A`), active state shifts to warm slate wash (`#E8DFD5`).
- **Icon Action Buttons:** `48px` circular buttons with soft drop shadow, centered SVG icon, and energetic hover bounce.

### Checkboxes (The "Task Popper")
- **Default State:** `24px` circular shape with a `2px` solid `#D6CCC2` border and `#FFFFFF` background.
- **Completed State:** Instant snap-fill to vibrant mint emerald (`#10B981`) with a white geometric checkmark icon. Triggers a quick scale micro-bounce (`scale(1.15)` returning to `scale(1)`).
- **Associated Text Behavior:** Checked tasks dim to secondary charcoal (`#716E69`) with an animated strike-through line.

### Task Cards
- Built using surface level 1 (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px solid #E8DFD5` border and `1.25rem` (`20px`) border radius.
- Padding sits at `space-md` (`0.875rem`) vertical by `space-lg` (`1.25rem`) horizontal.
- Contains the left-aligned completion checkbox, centered task label with category chip, and an optional right-aligned streak badge or due-date indicator.

### Chips & Badges
- Strict full-pill boundary (`rounded-full`) with a height of `28px` or `24px`.
- Built as tinted fills (10% tint of parent token) with high-saturation foreground text:
  - **Category Project:** Tinted purple background (`#8B5CF6` at 12% opacity) with solid `#7C3AED` text.
  - **Streak Badge:** Tinted amber background (`#F59E0B` at 15% opacity) with solid `#D97706` text and flame emoji/icon.
  - **Today / Urgent:** Tinted coral background (`#FF5D38` at 12% opacity) with solid `#FF5D38` text.

### Circular Progress Ring
- SVG-driven stroke dial with background track (`#E8DFD5`) at `6px` stroke width and animated foreground track in Mint Emerald (`#10B981`) or Primary Coral (`#FF5D38`).
- Center content features bold headline numbers paired with uppercase micro-labels (`label-caps`).

### Text Inputs
- Generous touch fields (`52px` minimum height) with `rounded-2xl` corners, `#F4EFEA` background, and no border in resting state.
- **Focus State:** Background transitions to `#FFFFFF` with a `2px solid #FF5D38` halo outline and slight card elevation.
